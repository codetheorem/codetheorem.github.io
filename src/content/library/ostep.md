## Why this is on the reading desk

A foundation for questions that reappear in inference runtimes and databases: scheduling, address spaces, synchronization, and durable state. The free chapter structure makes it useful for focused study.

## A question to keep

Use a small experiment or a worked example to connect an abstraction to the mechanism that supports it.
