## Why this is on the reading desk

The question to carry into this paper is how memory allocation changes the amount of useful work a serving system can admit. Separate the memory-management mechanism from the conditions of its reported evaluation.

## A question to keep

Read alongside the inference manuscript’s chapter on memory and batching. A paper’s benchmark is evidence about its experiment, not a universal throughput promise.
