# Public manuscript review

## Basis and scope

The author identified Tokenomics as an internal product and supplied a sanitized engineering background specifically for the blog. That background authorizes a conceptual account, original pseudocode and illustrations, synthetic workloads, and public references. It explicitly excludes internal code, prompts, customer material, infrastructure, datasets, and unauthorized numerical results.

This is an editorial record of those boundaries, not a statement of employer approval or an independent audit of the internal system.

## Material included

- Broad areas of work and motivation supplied by the author.
- A policy-dependent conceptual lifecycle, not a function-level trace.
- Original pseudocode labeled as unrelated to proprietary source.
- Eight conceptual illustrations with explicit scope labels.
- Independently constructed arithmetic using synthetic workloads and invented prices.
- Public documentation for external caching concepts.

## Material excluded

- Proprietary code and internal repository structure.
- Exact routing classifiers, thresholds, compression algorithms, and cache configuration.
- Internal prompts, business rules, customer requests, or customer datasets.
- Provider credentials, internal endpoints, deployment names, or private infrastructure.
- Internal benchmark values, production statistics, or financial results, including résumé-derived performance figures previously recorded in the research notes.
- Unverified savings percentages from the earlier writing brief.

## Capability wording

Fingerprinting and shadow-mode work remain described as explored. Future directions are labeled as such. The caching taxonomy does not imply every listed cache type is enabled. The optimization equations are an engineering objective, not a claim of a deployed solver.

`article.md` and `article-public.md` are identical public-safe versions. No private manuscript containing additional implementation details is retained in this package. The website version has the same narrative with a presentation component for illustrations.
