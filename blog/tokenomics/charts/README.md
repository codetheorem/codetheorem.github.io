# Benchmark charts

No internal benchmark charts are included. The author explicitly excluded exact internal results and datasets from publication, and no authorized measured dataset was supplied.

The article uses tables and equations with synthetic workloads and invented prices. Those calculations are reproducible with `../assets/illustrative-calculations.mjs`. They demonstrate relationships, not measured Tokenomics performance.

`../diagrams/tradeoffs.svg` is a conceptual policy-selection diagram, not an empirical Pareto frontier. It must not be described as a benchmark chart.
